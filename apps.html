Our apps

Free apps for iPhone and Android, each made to do one job well. No accounts and no ads.

  • Trip Spending

    Split trip costs with friends

    Record what everyone paid, in any currency, split by percent or fraction, and see the fewest payments that settle the trip in the currency you choose.

    Support and privacy

  • Before You Land

    Plugs, money and time abroad

    See what's different from home in 243 countries: plugs and voltage, the local time, weather, exchange rates and emergency numbers, plus packing lists and a passport check. Works offline, in English and Traditional Chinese.

    Support and privacy

  • Restaurant Pick

    5 well-rated places near you

    Hungry and can't decide? Pick a cuisine and Restaurant Pick finds 5 well-rated places nearby, with what diners say and walking directions in your map app.

    Support and privacy

  • Meal Tracker

    Calories and sugar against your goals

    Snap a photo of your plate, scan a barcode or type it in, and see each day's calories and sugar against your own goals. Meals stay on your phone.

    Support and privacy

  • Stocks: Gain & Loss

    Your shares' gain or loss at a glance

    Record what you bought and sold in Hong Kong, the US, London and China, and see each stock's gain or loss and your total in the currency you choose, with an Excel report.

    For record-keeping only, not investment or tax advice. Prices from Yahoo Finance may be delayed.

    Support and privacy

  • HSI Trend

    Hang Seng Index moves by turnover

    See how strong each Hang Seng Index day really was: the move weighted by how busy the market was, with a 5-day trend, updated every trading evening.

    For information only, not investment advice. HSI Trend is not affiliated with Hang Seng Indexes Company or HKEX.

    Support and privacy
